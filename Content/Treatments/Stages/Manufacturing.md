Manufacturing answers the question: **can we make this medicine reliably, purely, and identically — first for the people in clinical trials, and in a way that can later grow to supply every patient?** Making a small amount of something in a research lab and making a great deal of it consistently are very different challenges, and this stage is about the second one — turning a lab recipe into a scalable production process run under a binding quality system that requires every step documented, every material tested, and every batch traceable. No person can receive the medicine until it is made this way.

The work depends on what the medicine is. A small molecule's chemical route is often redesigned for large equipment — fewer, safer steps, with impurities identified and driven below strict limits — and then run at scale, where reactions behave differently than in a flask. Antibodies and designed proteins are grown by living cells in large, tightly controlled tanks and then purified out of everything else. Each of the other modalities — peptides, conjugates, mRNA, oligonucleotides, viral vectors, editing components, cell products, oncolytic viruses, radiopharmaceuticals, microbiome products, vaccines — has its own specialized production and release process. The common thread is consistency: any batch must be as safe and effective as any other, proven by testing every batch and validating that the process reliably makes a product within specification.

**A deliberate simplification.** In reality manufacturing is not one step but a thread running through the rest of development: a first GMP batch is made for the early trials, the process is enlarged and refined as trials grow, and it is only fully validated at commercial scale around the time of approval. The map collapses all of this into one stage, placed where GMP-made material first has to exist — just before the first person is dosed. It is **not** about commercialization, marketing, or selling the drug.

The stage is done when there is a **scalable production process making the drug reliably under GMP**, supplying the clinical trials and ready to grow to commercial scale.
