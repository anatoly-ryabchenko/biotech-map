# Moderna

Moderna is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, and a pure-play messenger-RNA company. It rose to prominence with the Spikevax COVID-19 vaccine and has since broadened into respiratory and latent-virus vaccines (the RSV vaccine mRESVIA), individualised cancer immunotherapy (with Merck), and mRNA therapeutics for rare diseases that work by making the body produce a missing protein.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~5,000 staff · HQ Cambridge, MA, US (as of 2026)
