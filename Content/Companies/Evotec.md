# Evotec

Evotec is a German drug-discovery and development company headquartered in Hamburg, working mainly as a partner to other pharma and biotech companies. It provides integrated discovery services — from target identification through to clinical candidate — across many disease areas, and through its Just-Evotec Biologics unit also designs and manufactures biologics.

**Scale:** Public (Frankfurt / Nasdaq) · ~5,000 staff · HQ Hamburg, DE (as of 2026)
