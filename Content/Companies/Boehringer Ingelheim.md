# Boehringer Ingelheim

Boehringer Ingelheim is a German pharmaceutical company headquartered in Ingelheim am Rhein and one of the largest privately held (family-owned) drugmakers in the world. Its human-pharma business is strongest in cardiometabolic disease (Jardiance), respiratory disease (Ofev, Spevigo), oncology and CNS, built mainly on small molecules and biologics. Its BioXcellence unit is also a large contract manufacturer of biologics for other companies.

**Scale:** Private (family-owned) · ~54,000 staff · HQ Ingelheim, DE (as of 2026)
