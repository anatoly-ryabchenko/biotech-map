# Ionis

Ionis Pharmaceuticals is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Carlsbad, California, and the pioneer of antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) drugs — short single strands of modified DNA/RNA that bind a target RNA to block or correct it. Its medicines include Spinraza (nusinersen, for spinal muscular atrophy, with Biogen), Wainua/eplontersen (with AstraZeneca) and Tryngolza (olezarsen). It increasingly markets its own drugs rather than only licensing them.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~1,000 staff · HQ Carlsbad, CA, US (as of 2026)
