# Charles River

Charles River Laboratories is a US contract research organisation (CRO) headquartered in Wilmington, Massachusetts, best known for early-stage and preclinical drug development services. It supplies research models, runs discovery services, and is one of the largest providers of GLP safety and toxicology testing — the regulated animal studies a drug must pass before first-in-human trials. It also offers cell- and gene-therapy contract manufacturing.

**Scale:** Public (NYSE) · ~20,000 staff · HQ Wilmington, MA, US (as of 2026)
