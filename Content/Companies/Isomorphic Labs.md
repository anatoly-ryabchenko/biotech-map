# Isomorphic Labs

Isomorphic Labs is a British AI drug-design company headquartered in London, spun out of Google DeepMind and backed by Alphabet. It builds on AlphaFold — the AI system that predicts protein structures — to design small-molecule drugs computationally, and raised $600 million in its first external funding round in 2025. It works through partnerships with Eli Lilly and Novartis and is moving its own programmes toward the clinic.

**Scale:** Private (Alphabet-backed; $600M round, 2025) · ~200 staff · HQ London, UK (as of 2026)
