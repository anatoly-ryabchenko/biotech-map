# Bachem

Bachem is a Swiss company headquartered in Bubendorf and the leading independent manufacturer of peptides for the drug industry — the contractor many companies rely on to make peptide active ingredients (such as GLP-1 drugs) at scale. It has also expanded into oligonucleotide manufacturing. It supplies active ingredients to other companies rather than selling its own medicines.

**Scale:** Public (SIX Swiss Exchange) · ~2,500 staff · HQ Bubendorf, CH (as of 2026)
