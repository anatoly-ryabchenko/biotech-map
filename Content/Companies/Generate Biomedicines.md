# Generate Biomedicines

Generate Biomedicines is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Somerville, Massachusetts (founded by Flagship Pioneering), using generative AI to design protein therapeutics — antibodies and other proteins — from scratch rather than discovering them by screening. Its platform learns the rules of protein structure and generates novel sequences to order; its first programmes are entering the clinic, and it has research deals with companies such as Novartis and Amgen.

**Scale:** Private (Flagship-founded; raised >$700M, incl. Series C) · ~400 staff · HQ Somerville, MA, US (as of 2026)
