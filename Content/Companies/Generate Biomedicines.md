# Generate Biomedicines

Generate Biomedicines is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Somerville, Massachusetts (founded by Flagship Pioneering), using generative AI to design protein therapeutics — antibodies and other proteins — from scratch rather than discovering them by screening. Its platform learns the rules of protein structure and generates novel sequences to order; its most advanced programme, the long-acting anti-TSLP antibody GB-0895 for severe asthma, is in Phase 3 trials, and it has research deals with companies such as Novartis and Amgen. It listed on Nasdaq in 2026.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq, listed 2026) · ~400 staff · HQ Somerville, MA, US (as of 2026)
