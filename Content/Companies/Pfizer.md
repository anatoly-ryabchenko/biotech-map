# Pfizer

Pfizer is a US pharmaceutical company headquartered in New York City, with a broad portfolio across oncology, vaccines, internal medicine, inflammation/immunology and rare disease. It co-developed the Comirnaty COVID-19 mRNA vaccine with BioNTech and makes the Prevnar pneumococcal vaccine. Its 2023 acquisition of Seagen made it a major player in antibody-drug conjugates, and it also develops small molecules, antibodies, therapeutic proteins and gene therapies.

**Scale:** Public (NYSE) · ~80,000 staff · HQ New York, US (as of 2026)
