# Ariceum

Ariceum Therapeutics is a private, clinical-stage radiopharmaceutical company headquartered in Berlin, Germany (with operations across Switzerland, the UK, Australia and the US). Its lead candidate, satoreotide, is a molecule that blocks the SSTR2 receptor — overexpressed on aggressive neuroendocrine tumours and small-cell lung cancer — and carries a therapeutic isotope (lutetium-177 or actinium-225) to those tumours.

**Scale:** Private (Series A, ~€48M raised) · ~20 staff · HQ Berlin, DE (as of 2026)
