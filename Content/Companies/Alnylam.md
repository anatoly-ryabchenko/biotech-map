# Alnylam

Alnylam is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, and the pioneer of RNA interference (RNAi) therapeutics — short interfering RNA (siRNA) drugs that silence disease-causing genes. It has several approved medicines (Onpattro, Givlaari, Oxlumo and Amvuttra) and pioneered GalNAc conjugation, which delivers siRNA efficiently to liver cells with simple injections.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~2,500 staff · HQ Cambridge, MA, US (as of 2026)
