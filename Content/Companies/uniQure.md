# uniQure

uniQure is a gene-therapy company headquartered in Amsterdam, the Netherlands (with operations in Lexington, Massachusetts). It develops AAV (adeno-associated virus) gene therapies and made Hemgenix, the first approved gene therapy for haemophilia B (commercialised with CSL). Its lead clinical programme, AMT-130, is a gene therapy for Huntington's disease delivered directly into the brain.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~600 staff · HQ Amsterdam, NL (as of 2026)
