# Arvinas

Arvinas is a US biotechnology company headquartered in New Haven, Connecticut, and a pioneer of targeted protein degradation using PROTACs — small molecules that tag a disease-causing protein for the cell's own disposal machinery rather than just blocking it. Its lead programme, vepdegestrant (an oestrogen-receptor degrader for breast cancer, developed with Pfizer), was approved by the FDA in May 2026 as Veppanu — the first PROTAC approved for any disease; it also has CNS programmes.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~400 staff · HQ New Haven, CT, US (as of 2026)
