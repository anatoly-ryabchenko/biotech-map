# Catalent

Catalent is a contract development and manufacturing organisation (CDMO) headquartered in Somerset, New Jersey, taken private by Novo Holdings in 2024. It specialises in formulation, drug-delivery technologies and sterile fill-finish across small molecules and biologics, and also manufactures cell and gene therapies and mRNA. It develops and makes products for other companies rather than selling its own medicines.

**Scale:** Private (Novo Holdings, acquired 2024) · ~18,000 staff · HQ Somerset, NJ, US (as of 2026)
