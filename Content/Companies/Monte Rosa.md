# Monte Rosa

Monte Rosa Therapeutics is a biotechnology company headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, with research operations in Basel, Switzerland, specialising in molecular glue degraders — small molecules that "glue" a disease-causing protein to the cell's disposal machinery so it is destroyed. Its QuEEN discovery engine screens for such glues; programmes span oncology and immunology (for example a VAV1 degrader).

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~250 staff · HQ Boston, MA, US (R&D in Basel, CH) (as of 2026)
