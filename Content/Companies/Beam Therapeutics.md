# Beam Therapeutics

Beam Therapeutics is a US gene-editing company headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, specialising in base editing — a precise form of CRISPR editing that changes a single DNA letter without cutting both strands of the double helix, reducing the risk of unwanted rearrangements. Its programmes include an ex-vivo treatment for sickle cell disease (BEAM-101) and an in-vivo treatment for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency (BEAM-302).

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~700 staff · HQ Cambridge, MA, US (as of 2026)
