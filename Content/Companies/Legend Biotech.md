# Legend Biotech

Legend Biotech is a cell-therapy company headquartered in Somerset, New Jersey, with major operations in Nanjing, China (it was founded as a subsidiary of GenScript). It developed Carvykti (ciltacabtagene autoleucel), a CAR-T cell therapy for multiple myeloma marketed with Johnson & Johnson, which reprogrammes a patient's own T cells to attack cancer cells carrying the BCMA marker.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~2,500 staff · HQ Somerset, NJ, US (as of 2026)
