# Roche

Roche is a Swiss healthcare company headquartered in Basel, made up of a Pharmaceuticals division and a Diagnostics division. Its US pharma arm, Genentech (South San Francisco), pioneered the antibody-drug industry and remains its discovery engine. Roche's medicines span oncology, neuroscience, immunology, ophthalmology and haematology, and include antibodies (Herceptin, Avastin, Ocrevus), antibody-drug conjugates (Kadcyla, Polivy), small molecules, and a gene therapy (Luxturna, via Spark Therapeutics). Only its treatment activities are mapped here; diagnostics fall outside this vault.

**Scale:** Public (SIX Swiss Exchange) · ~100,000 staff · HQ Basel, CH (as of 2026)
