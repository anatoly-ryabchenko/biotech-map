# Insilico Medicine

Insilico Medicine is a generative-AI drug-discovery company headquartered in Hong Kong (with sites in the US, mainland China and the UAE), which listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in December 2025. Its Pharma.AI platform spans target discovery, generative chemistry and trial design. Its lead drug, rentosertib (a TNIK inhibitor for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis), is cited as the first clinical proof-of-concept for an AI-discovered, AI-designed medicine and entered a Phase 3 trial in China in 2026.

**Scale:** Public (HKEX, listed 2025) · ~300 staff · HQ Hong Kong (as of 2026)
