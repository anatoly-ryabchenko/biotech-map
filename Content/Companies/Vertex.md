# Vertex

Vertex Pharmaceuticals is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, which built its business on small-molecule drugs that treat the underlying cause of cystic fibrosis (Trikafta and predecessors). It has since diversified into a non-opioid pain medicine (Journavx) and into advanced therapies: Casgevy, the first approved CRISPR gene-editing medicine (for sickle cell disease and beta-thalassemia, developed with CRISPR Therapeutics), and a stem-cell-derived islet-cell therapy for type 1 diabetes.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~6,000 staff · HQ Boston, MA, US (as of 2026)
