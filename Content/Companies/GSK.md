# GSK

GSK (GlaxoSmithKline) is a British pharmaceutical company headquartered in London, focused on infectious disease, HIV, immunology/respiratory and oncology since spinning off its consumer-health arm (Haleon) in 2022. It is one of the world's largest vaccine makers (Shingrix for shingles, Arexvy for RSV) and also develops small molecules, antibodies, an antibody-drug conjugate (Blenrep), and mRNA vaccines for influenza and COVID-19 (which it controls under a 2024 licensing agreement with CureVac).

**Scale:** Public (LSE / NYSE) · ~68,000 staff · HQ London, UK (as of 2026)
