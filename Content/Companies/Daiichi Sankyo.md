# Daiichi Sankyo

Daiichi Sankyo is a Japanese pharmaceutical company headquartered in Tokyo, now best known as a leader in antibody-drug conjugates. Its proprietary DXd linker-payload platform underpins Enhertu and Datroway (both partnered with AstraZeneca) and a deep pipeline of ADCs (including collaborations with Merck). It also develops small molecules and antibodies across oncology and other areas.

**Scale:** Public (Tokyo Stock Exchange) · ~18,000 staff · HQ Tokyo, JP (as of 2026)
