# C4 Therapeutics

C4 Therapeutics is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Watertown, Massachusetts, developing targeted protein degraders, primarily in oncology. Its TORPEDO platform builds both two-headed degraders (which bridge a target to a disposal enzyme) and smaller monofunctional degraders; lead programmes include cemsidomide and an EGFR-mutant degrader.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~200 staff · HQ Watertown, MA, US (as of 2026)
