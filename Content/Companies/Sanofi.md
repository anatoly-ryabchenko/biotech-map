# Sanofi

Sanofi is a French pharmaceutical company headquartered in Paris, strong in immunology (Dupixent, developed with Regeneron), vaccines (its Sanofi Pasteur arm is among the world's largest flu-vaccine makers), rare disease and oncology. Its rare-disease franchise, built on the former Genzyme, makes it a leader in enzyme-replacement therapeutic proteins. It also runs an mRNA vaccine effort built on its acquisition of Translate Bio.

**Scale:** Public (Euronext Paris / Nasdaq) · ~83,000 staff · HQ Paris, FR (as of 2026)
