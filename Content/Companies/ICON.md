# ICON

ICON plc is an Irish clinical contract research organisation (CRO) headquartered in Dublin, one of the largest in the world. It designs, manages and runs clinical trials across all phases for pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies, and provides related services such as laboratory testing, biostatistics and regulatory support.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~40,000 staff · HQ Dublin, IE (as of 2026)
