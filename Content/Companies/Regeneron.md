# Regeneron

Regeneron is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Tarrytown, New York, built around its VelocImmune platform for discovering fully human antibodies in genetically engineered mice. Its products include the antibodies Dupixent (with Sanofi) and Libtayo, and Eylea (aflibercept), an Fc-fusion "trap" protein for eye disease. It partners with Alnylam on siRNA and with Intellia on in-vivo CRISPR gene editing, and develops gene therapies for hearing loss.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~15,000 staff · HQ Tarrytown, NY, US (as of 2026)
