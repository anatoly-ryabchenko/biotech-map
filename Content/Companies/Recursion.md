# Recursion

Recursion is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah, that runs drug discovery as an industrial-scale data operation: automated labs generate enormous image-based datasets ("maps of biology") that machine-learning models mine for targets and drug candidates. Its 2024 merger with the UK's Exscientia added AI-driven precision molecule design. It has several small molecules in clinical trials but no approved product yet.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~800 staff · HQ Salt Lake City, UT, US (as of 2026)
