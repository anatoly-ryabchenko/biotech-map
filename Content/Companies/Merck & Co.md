# Merck & Co.

Merck & Co. (known as MSD outside the US and Canada, and distinct from Germany's Merck KGaA) is a US pharmaceutical company headquartered in Rahway, New Jersey. Its biggest product is the cancer immunotherapy antibody Keytruda (pembrolizumab); it is also a major vaccine maker (Gardasil for HPV) and develops small molecules, antibody-drug conjugates (partnered with Daiichi Sankyo) and therapeutic proteins.

**Scale:** Public (NYSE) · ~70,000 staff · HQ Rahway, NJ, US (as of 2026)
