# Lonza

Lonza is a Swiss company headquartered in Basel and the world's largest contract development and manufacturing organisation (CDMO) — it makes medicines for other companies rather than selling its own. Its capabilities span antibodies and proteins, small-molecule APIs, cell and gene therapies, mRNA, and antibody-drug conjugates, plus formulation of poorly soluble small molecules; in 2026 it agreed to sell its capsules and health-ingredients business to become a pure contract manufacturer. This makes it a manufacturing backbone for much of the industry.

**Scale:** Public (SIX Swiss Exchange) · ~18,000 staff · HQ Basel, CH (as of 2026)
