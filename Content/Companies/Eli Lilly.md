# Eli Lilly

Eli Lilly is a US pharmaceutical company headquartered in Indianapolis, a leader in diabetes and obesity (the GLP-1/GIP peptide tirzepatide — Mounjaro and Zepbound) and in Alzheimer's, oncology and immunology. Alongside peptides it develops small molecules, antibodies, insulin and other therapeutic proteins, and through acquisitions has built radiopharmaceutical (POINT Biopharma, Aktis), gene-therapy (Prevail) and in-body gene-editing (Verve Therapeutics, 2025) capabilities; in 2026 it agreed to buy Kelonia, which engineers CAR-T cells inside the body.

**Scale:** Public (NYSE) · ~47,000 staff · HQ Indianapolis, US (as of 2026)
