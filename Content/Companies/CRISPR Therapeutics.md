# CRISPR Therapeutics

CRISPR Therapeutics is a gene-editing company headquartered in Zug, Switzerland (with major operations in Boston), co-founded by CRISPR pioneer Emmanuelle Charpentier. With Vertex it developed Casgevy, the first approved CRISPR/Cas9 medicine, which edits a patient's own blood stem cells outside the body to treat sickle cell disease and beta-thalassemia. Its pipeline also includes in-vivo editing and gene-edited "off-the-shelf" (allogeneic) CAR-T cell therapies.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~500 staff · HQ Zug, CH (as of 2026)
