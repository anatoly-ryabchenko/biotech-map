# WuXi AppTec

WuXi AppTec is a Chinese contract research, development and manufacturing organisation (CRDMO) headquartered in Shanghai, offering integrated services across the drug pipeline — from discovery chemistry and biology, through lab testing, to manufacturing of small molecules, peptides and oligonucleotides. It sold its US and UK cell- and gene-therapy manufacturing unit (WuXi Advanced Therapies) to the investment firm Altaris in 2025. (Its sister company WuXi Biologics, separately listed, covers antibody and protein manufacturing.) It works as a contractor for thousands of drug companies worldwide.

**Scale:** Public (HKEX / Shanghai) · ~38,000 staff · HQ Shanghai, CN (as of 2026)
