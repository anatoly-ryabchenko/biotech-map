# WuXi AppTec

WuXi AppTec is a Chinese contract research, development and manufacturing organisation (CRDMO) headquartered in Shanghai, offering integrated services across the drug pipeline — from discovery chemistry and biology, through lab testing, to manufacturing of small molecules, peptides, oligonucleotides and cell and gene therapies. (Its sister company WuXi Biologics, separately listed, covers antibody and protein manufacturing.) It works as a contractor for thousands of drug companies worldwide.

**Scale:** Public (HKEX / Shanghai) · ~38,000 staff · HQ Shanghai, CN (as of 2026)
