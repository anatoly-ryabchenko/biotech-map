# Amgen

Amgen is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Thousand Oaks, California, and one of the pioneers of the recombinant-protein drug industry (epoetin alfa, filgrastim, etanercept). Today it is strongest in antibodies (including bispecific T-cell engagers such as Blincyto), therapeutic proteins and biosimilars, with a growing small-molecule oncology franchise (the KRAS inhibitor Lumakras) and an siRNA programme (olpasiran, with Arrowhead).

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~28,000 staff · HQ Thousand Oaks, CA, US (as of 2026)
