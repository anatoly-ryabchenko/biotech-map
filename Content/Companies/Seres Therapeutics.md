# Seres Therapeutics

Seres Therapeutics is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, a pioneer of microbiome therapeutics — medicines made of defined communities of bacteria intended to restore a healthy gut ecosystem. It developed Vowst, the first FDA-approved oral microbiome drug (for recurrent *C. difficile* infection), whose rights it later sold to Nestlé; it now focuses on a live biotherapeutic (SER-155) to prevent infections in vulnerable patients.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~100 staff · HQ Cambridge, MA, US (as of 2026)
