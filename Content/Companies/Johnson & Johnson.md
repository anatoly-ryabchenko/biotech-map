# Johnson & Johnson

Johnson & Johnson is a US healthcare company headquartered in New Brunswick, New Jersey. Its Innovative Medicine division (formerly Janssen) is strongest in oncology, immunology, neuroscience and infectious disease, with antibodies (Darzalex, Stelara, Tremfya), small molecules, and the CAR-T cell therapy Carvykti (developed with Legend Biotech). Only its medicines are mapped here; its MedTech division falls outside this vault.

**Scale:** Public (NYSE) · ~140,000 staff (group) · HQ New Brunswick, NJ, US (as of 2026)
