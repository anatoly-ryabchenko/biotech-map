# Replimune

Replimune is a biotechnology company headquartered in Woburn, Massachusetts (with roots in Oxford, UK), developing oncolytic viruses — viruses engineered to infect and burst tumour cells while alerting the immune system to the cancer. Its lead product, RP1 (in combination with nivolumab) for advanced melanoma, is under FDA review after a 2025 complete response letter and resubmission; RP2 and RP3 follow behind.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~300 staff · HQ Woburn, MA, US (as of 2026)
