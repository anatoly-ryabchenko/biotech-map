# Replimune

Replimune is a biotechnology company headquartered in Woburn, Massachusetts (with roots in Oxford, UK), developing oncolytic viruses — viruses engineered to infect and burst tumour cells while alerting the immune system to the cancer. Its lead product, RP1 (vusolimogene oderparepvec, given with nivolumab for advanced melanoma), won FDA accelerated approval as Tudriqev in August 2026 after two earlier rejections, with a confirmatory Phase 3 trial still to report; RP2 and RP3 follow behind.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~300 staff · HQ Woburn, MA, US (as of 2026)
