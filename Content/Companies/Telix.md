# Telix

Telix Pharmaceuticals is an Australian radiopharmaceutical company headquartered in Melbourne, one of the largest dedicated radiopharma players outside the big pharma companies. It is commercial-stage in diagnostic imaging agents (such as the prostate-cancer imaging agent Illuccix) and is developing a pipeline of therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals — molecules that carry a radioactive isotope to a tumour to destroy it — for prostate, kidney, brain and other cancers. Only its therapeutic activities are mapped here.

**Scale:** Public (ASX / Nasdaq) · ~700 staff · HQ Melbourne, AU (as of 2026)
