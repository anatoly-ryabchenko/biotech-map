# Bristol Myers Squibb

Bristol Myers Squibb (BMS) is a US pharmaceutical company headquartered in Princeton, New Jersey, focused on oncology, haematology, immunology and cardiovascular disease. It makes the checkpoint-inhibitor antibody Opdivo, the CAR-T cell therapies Breyanzi and Abecma, and the cereblon-based protein degraders (molecular glues) Revlimid and Pomalyst. Its 2024 acquisition of RayzeBio added a radiopharmaceutical (actinium-based) pipeline.

**Scale:** Public (NYSE) · ~34,000 staff · HQ Princeton, NJ, US (as of 2026)
