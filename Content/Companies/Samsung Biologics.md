# Samsung Biologics

Samsung Biologics is a South Korean contract development and manufacturing organisation (CDMO) headquartered in Incheon, and one of the largest biologics manufacturers in the world by capacity. It manufactures antibodies and other therapeutic proteins for other companies, offers sterile fill-finish, and has expanded into antibody-drug conjugate manufacturing.

**Scale:** Public (KRX) · ~5,000 staff · HQ Incheon, KR (as of 2026)
