# argenx

argenx is a Belgian-Dutch biotechnology company with operational headquarters in Ghent, Belgium, focused on antibody therapeutics for severe autoimmune diseases. Its lead product, Vyvgart (efgartigimod), is an engineered antibody fragment that blocks the FcRn receptor to lower harmful antibody levels, approved for myasthenia gravis and other conditions. Its antibodies come from the SIMPLE Antibody platform derived from llama immune systems.

**Scale:** Public (Euronext Brussels / Nasdaq) · ~2,000 staff · HQ Ghent, BE (as of 2026)
