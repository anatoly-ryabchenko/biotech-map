# Novo Nordisk

Novo Nordisk is a Danish pharmaceutical company headquartered in Bagsværd, the world leader in diabetes and obesity care. It is best known for its GLP-1 peptide drugs (semaglutide — Ozempic and Wegovy) and for insulin and other therapeutic proteins. It also develops oral small molecules, siRNA drugs (via its Dicerna acquisition), and stem-cell-derived therapies for type 1 diabetes. A 2025 restructuring reduced its headcount from a peak near 78,000.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq Copenhagen / NYSE) · ~70,000 staff · HQ Bagsværd, DK (as of 2026)
