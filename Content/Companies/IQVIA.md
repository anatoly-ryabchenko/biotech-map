# IQVIA

IQVIA is a US company headquartered in Durham, North Carolina, that combines one of the world's largest clinical contract research organisations (CRO) with a major healthcare data and analytics business. On the development side it designs and runs clinical trials of all phases on behalf of drug companies, and provides regulatory and real-world-evidence services.

**Scale:** Public (NYSE) · ~88,000 staff · HQ Durham, NC, US (as of 2026)
