# BioNTech

BioNTech is a German biotechnology company headquartered in Mainz, best known for co-developing the Comirnaty COVID-19 mRNA vaccine with Pfizer. It is building a diversified oncology company around mRNA cancer immunotherapies (including individualised neoantigen vaccines), bispecific antibodies (BNT327), antibody-drug conjugates, and cell therapies. In January 2026 it completed its acquisition of fellow mRNA company CureVac, strengthening its mRNA design, delivery and manufacturing capabilities.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~7,500 staff · HQ Mainz, DE (as of 2026)
