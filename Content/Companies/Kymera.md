# Kymera

Kymera Therapeutics is a US biotechnology company headquartered in Watertown, Massachusetts, developing targeted protein degraders, with a focus on oral degraders for immunology and inflammation (such as IRAK4 and STAT6 degraders) as an alternative to injected antibodies. Its degraders are designed to remove signalling proteins that conventional small molecules struggle to drug.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~400 staff · HQ Watertown, MA, US (as of 2026)
