# MaaT Pharma

MaaT Pharma is a French biotechnology company headquartered in Lyon, developing microbiome therapeutics built from full, donor-derived microbial ecosystems rather than a few defined strains. Its lead product, MaaT013 (Xervyteg), treats graft-versus-host disease — a serious complication in which transplanted immune cells attack the patient — in blood-cancer patients; in 2026 European regulators declined to approve it on single-arm trial data, and the company plans a randomized Phase 3 trial.

**Scale:** Public (Euronext Paris) · ~100 staff · HQ Lyon, FR (as of 2026)
