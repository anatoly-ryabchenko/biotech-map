# Cradle

Cradle is a Dutch-Swiss biotechnology company headquartered in Amsterdam, with an office in Zurich, that builds generative-AI software for protein engineering. Rather than developing its own drugs, it provides design tools that let other companies' scientists improve a protein's stability, expression or binding, and runs an in-house wet lab to generate the data its models learn from. It raised a $73 million Series B in 2024.

**Scale:** Private (Series B, 2024) · ~60 staff · HQ Amsterdam, NL (as of 2026)
