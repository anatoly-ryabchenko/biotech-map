# Genmab

Genmab is a Danish biotechnology company headquartered in Copenhagen, specialising in therapeutic antibodies. It invented several widely used antibody-engineering platforms — DuoBody (bispecifics), HexaBody (enhanced effector function) and others — and either markets or earns royalties on antibodies such as Darzalex (daratumumab), Epkinly (epcoritamab, a bispecific) and the antibody-drug conjugate Tivdak. Manufacturing and late commercialisation are usually done with partners.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq Copenhagen / Nasdaq) · ~2,500 staff · HQ Copenhagen, DK (as of 2026)
