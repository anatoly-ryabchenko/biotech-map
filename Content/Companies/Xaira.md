# Xaira

Xaira Therapeutics is a US biotechnology company headquartered in San Francisco, launched in 2024 with about $1 billion in committed funding (from ARCH Venture Partners and Foresite Labs). It combines machine learning for drug discovery with de-novo protein design built on the RFdiffusion and RFantibody models from David Baker's lab, and is led by former Genentech research head Marc Tessier-Lavigne. It is still pre-clinical, building its pipeline.

**Scale:** Private (~$1B launch funding, 2024) · ~180 staff · HQ San Francisco, CA, US (as of 2026)
