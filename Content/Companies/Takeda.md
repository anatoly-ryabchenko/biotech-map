# Takeda

Takeda is a Japanese pharmaceutical company headquartered in Tokyo, the largest in Japan and global in reach (with a major US hub in Cambridge, Massachusetts). It is strongest in gastroenterology, rare disease, neuroscience, oncology and plasma-derived therapies. Its 2019 acquisition of Shire made it a leader in plasma-derived and recombinant protein therapeutics (immunoglobulins, clotting factors); it also makes small molecules, antibodies and the dengue vaccine Qdenga.

**Scale:** Public (Tokyo / NYSE) · ~49,000 staff · HQ Tokyo, JP (as of 2026)
