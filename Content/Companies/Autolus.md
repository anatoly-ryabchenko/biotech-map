# Autolus

Autolus Therapeutics is a British cell-therapy company headquartered in London, UK, developing CAR-T therapies built with finely engineered receptors intended to improve safety and persistence. Its first approved product, Aucatzyl (obecabtagene autoleucel), treats adults with relapsed or refractory B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukaemia.

**Scale:** Public (Nasdaq) · ~700 staff · HQ London, UK (as of 2026)
