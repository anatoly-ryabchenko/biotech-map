# Bayer

Bayer is a German life-sciences company headquartered in Leverkusen, with a Pharmaceuticals division (alongside Crop Science and Consumer Health; only its pharma activities are mapped here). Its medicines are strongest in cardiology (Xarelto), oncology (Nubeqa, and the radiopharmaceutical Xofigo) and haematology (recombinant factor VIII). Through acquisitions it has built advanced-therapy platforms: AskBio for AAV gene therapy and BlueRock Therapeutics for stem-cell-derived (iPSC) cell therapies.

**Scale:** Public (Frankfurt / Xetra) · ~90,000 staff (group) · HQ Leverkusen, DE (as of 2026)
